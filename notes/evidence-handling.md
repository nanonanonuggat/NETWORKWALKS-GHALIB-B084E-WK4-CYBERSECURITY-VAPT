## Naming Convention
EV-[NUMBER]_[PHASE]_[DESCRIPTION]_[DATE]_[TIME].[EXTENSION]

## Evidence Rules
- Assign a unique Evidence ID.
- Record date and time.
- Keep original evidence secure.
- Redact sensitive information in screenshots.
- Do not store unnecessary patient information.
- Do not expose credentials or session tokens.
- Keep testing logs for report preparation.
