
# Sensitive Data Handling Policy

## Project Information
- Project: Mediroza General Hospital
- Batch: B084
- Engagement: Black-box Penetration Testing

## 1. Patient Information
- Do not expose patient personal information.
- Avoid unnecessary copying of patient records.
- Redact patient names, IDs, and medical details
  in screenshots and reports.
- Retain only evidence necessary for the assignment.

## 2. Credentials and Sessions
- Do not include passwords in reports.
- Do not expose session tokens or cookies.
- Redact sensitive authentication information.

## 3. Employee Information
- Treat employee salary information as confidential.
- Avoid unnecessary collection of personal details.
- Use redacted evidence in documentation.

## 4. Shareholder Information
- Treat shareholder details as confidential.
- Record only information necessary to demonstrate
  the identified exposure.
- Redact unnecessary personal information.

## 5. Evidence Protection
- Store evidence inside the project directory.
- Restrict folder permissions.
- Keep original evidence secure.
- Do not share sensitive evidence publicly.

## 6. Reporting
- Use redacted screenshots.
- Document vulnerabilities and their impact.
- Avoid including unnecessary sensitive data.
- Provide remediation recommendations.

## 7. Data Handling Principles
- Data minimization.
- Confidentiality.
- Controlled access.
- Secure evidence storage.
- Responsible reporting.

## Notes
Sensitive information discovered during testing
must be handled carefully and only retained
as necessary for the authorized assignment.
